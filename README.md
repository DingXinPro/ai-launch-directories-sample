# AI Launch Directories

**A researched starting sample of places you can actually submit an AI or software product.**

> **Looking for the full researched list of 348 sites? Get it here: [https://dingster13.gumroad.com/l/ai-launch-directories](https://dingster13.gumroad.com/l/ai-launch-directories)**

This repository publishes a **free 20-site sample**, with official homepages, submit URLs, categories, and a short note on what each site is actually good for. The listings were checked on **19 November 2026**. Two parked or broken domains were dropped from a larger queue instead of inventing rows to hit a round number.

This sample does **not** invent Moz Domain Authority or Ahrefs Domain Rating. When a domain appears in the open [Tranco top 1 million](https://tranco-list.eu/), that rank is shown; otherwise the field is left blank. The Chrome Web Store rank is omitted, because a storefront URL can inherit a parent-domain rank and would be misleading here.

A listing does not automatically bring traffic or sales. Always confirm the current rules on the official page before you publish a badge or start any paid process.

## Why this exists

Many public “500 AI directories” posts are padded with dead links and made-up authority scores. This sample is a shorter, checked starting queue: launch boards, community boards, software review platforms, AI directories, startup databases, and one store.

The machine-readable version of the same 20 rows, including verification notes and source lines, is in [`sample-20.csv`](./sample-20.csv).

## Quick start

1. Scan the tables below, grouped by category.
2. Open the official homepage or submit URL — do not rely on a third-party copy of the rules.
3. If you want the rows in a spreadsheet, download [`sample-20.csv`](./sample-20.csv).
4. Pricing in the CSV is marked `free`, `freemium`, or `unknown`. **Unknown means no reliable official basis was found, not that the site is free.**

## Method

- Unique sites only. The larger queue was 348 unique listings after two parked or broken domains were removed.
- Official URLs only. No third-party “directory of directories” was used as the source of truth.
- Authority is either a real Tranco rank or a qualitative tier in the full dataset. Fake DA/DR scores are not used.
- Fees are marked unknown unless there was a reasonable official basis. Please re-check before you pay for a listing or a badge.
- A homepage that returned a bot-protection or challenge page is noted in the CSV; that is not treated as a successful content check.
